Work

What I do with people, and how I got here.

Therapeutic Coach

Since April 2022, and UK and Europe representative for 5th Place

One to one since April 2022 with people living with bipolar: patterns, stability, what to do with a feeling on an ordinary day. Trained by Frances Masters at the Integrated Coaching Academy, in the Fusion model. With 5th Place I take Shape of Emotion and the Emotional Fitness Model to organisations, schools and communities. I first learned to ask what shape a feeling is at my own dining table.

Recovery College Peer Trainer

Northamptonshire Healthcare NHS Foundation Trust, since February 2026

I run Living with Bipolar, for people living with bipolar and those around them, with more dates being published soon. I’d already co-delivered it here before I joined. I also co-facilitate other courses and workshops, and co-write what gets taught, on the principle that services are best designed and delivered by people who’ve used them.

Emotional fitness

Trained by Matthew Green and Chantal Dawtrey, and their UK and Europe representative. Shape of Emotion and the Emotional Fitness Model, for organisations, schools and communities.

Speaking

The International Society for Bipolar Disorders in Chiba. Two hundred young students, and a talk called What if nothing is wrong with you.
