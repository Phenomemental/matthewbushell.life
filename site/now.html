At the moment

In Northampton, on UK time.

Every night in my book opens the same way. The hour, the body, what’s in the room. It’s 21:07. Very little pain today. Haven is unsettled beside me. Dr Louie Gardiner’s Presence in Action taught me to do that on purpose. What are the facts? What are the feelings? What are the fictions? What am I here for? Ask, and the next thing changes.

The five pillars I learned with Matthew Green and Chantal Dawtrey at 5th Place keep me company through the day: thinking, feeling, moving, nourishing, relating.

All of it is in this hour with me, and the next thing starts from here. So here is what my days are made of now, and the place each one lives in.

Community

LERO Coordinator

The Forward Trust, North Northamptonshire, since July 2026

A Lived Experience Recovery Organisation is recovery run by the people who’ve lived it. I support their development across the county. The people who come forward are experts by experience, and the culture gets set in how we treat each other. I’m also on ImROC’s LERO mentoring programme, and a member and volunteer of Community Recovery, a distinct organisation here with its own leaders.

Cyberspace

Digital social innovation

Apps and tools for different people, and Pscale with David Pinto

Nesta’s Our Tech, Our Future talks about “tech as a tool, not an end”. That’s how I work. I make apps and tools with AI, with the people they’re for, around what matters in those people’s lives. A website and an app for a recovery community, co-produced with its leaders, with privacy first. An offer to Lived Experience Recovery Organisations, where a member keeps their own days, and with their yes those days become their organisation’s evidence. Sundial, where you talk, it keeps your day with you, and nothing leaves the phone. And Pscale, with David Pinto, where people keep their own account in their own words. It all draws on twenty-six years of taking a technology into a room of people and making it work for them.
